What are Yogasanas?

A Technology for Inner Transformation

Yogasanas are a set of 36 powerful postures to enable the system to sustain higher dimensions of energy. These are not mere physical exercises — they are a means to explore and experience dimensions of life which are not normally available to human beings.

In the yogic understanding, the human body is a microcosm of the universe. The 36 postures of Isha Yogasanas are designed to transform the body from being an obstacle to a doorway — opening possibilities of perception and experience that go far beyond the ordinary.

At Yogartha, these classical postures are taught in their full depth — with awareness of breath, energy, and inner experience — not as mere physical stretches but as a profound technology for human wellbeing.

Benefits

Why Practice Yogasanas

Transforms the Body

Enables the physical body to sustain higher levels of energy by increasing flexibility, strength, and the overall health of the musculoskeletal system.

Enhances Awareness

When practiced with the right intent and awareness, each posture becomes a vehicle for inner exploration rather than a mere physical exercise.

Balances the Energy Body

Each of the 36 asanas is designed to work on specific dimensions of the energy body (pranamayakosha), bringing balance and flow to the pranic system.

Sharpens the Mind

A stable, well-aligned physical body creates the foundation for a clear, focused, and penetrating mind — essential for all aspects of life.

Creates Wellbeing

Regular practice brings about a state of ease, health, and inner wellbeing that goes well beyond what the physical practice alone would suggest.

Prepares for Higher Practices

Yogasanas create a strong and receptive body — a prerequisite for deeper kriyas, pranayama, and meditative practices.
