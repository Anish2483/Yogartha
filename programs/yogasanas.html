What is Yogasanas?

More Than Stretching — A Transformative Science

The word "asana" means a posture. But in the Isha tradition, asanas are far more than physical stretches — they are precise scientific tools to align your body, mind, and energy. When practiced correctly, they can make the body a powerful foundation for inner growth.

Yogasanas at Yogartha are taught exactly as Sadhguru has structured them: with perfect alignment, meditative awareness, and understanding of the energy body. Each posture is held with consciousness, allowing the pranic (life energy) body to become more fluid and vibrant.

These are not gym exercises. The difference is in the awareness, the breath integration, and the intention — making the practice not just physically rewarding but deeply transformative.
