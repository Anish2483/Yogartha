What is Yoga for Kids?

The Gift of Yoga from Childhood

Isha Yoga for Children is a specially crafted program that introduces children to the yogic way of life. It is designed to nurture children’s natural exuberance, creativity, and capacity for inner stillness — channeling these qualities through age-appropriate yogic practices.

The program combines physical yogic practices with breath awareness, simple meditative processes, and Yogic stories and mythology — making it engaging, fun, and deeply meaningful for children. Every element is calibrated to suit the developmental stage of the child.

Introducing yoga at a young age creates a strong foundation of physical health, mental clarity, emotional balance, and inner wellbeing that can support a child throughout their entire life. The seeds planted in childhood often become the most enduring gifts of all.

Benefits

Why Yoga for Children

Physical Development

Builds strength, flexibility, coordination, and body awareness in children during the critical years of physical development.

Focus & Concentration

Yogic practices dramatically improve a child’s ability to focus, concentrate, and retain information — directly supporting academic performance.

Emotional Balance

Teaches children tools for managing their emotions with greater awareness and equanimity — a skill that serves them lifelong.

Builds Confidence

As children master new practices and experience their body’s capability, they develop a natural self-confidence and sense of competence.

Inner Joy

Children naturally gravitate toward joy. Yoga for Kids harnesses this natural quality, deepening it and making it more stable and lasting.

Lifelong Foundation

Early introduction to yoga creates habits, awareness, and tools that support physical, mental, and emotional wellbeing throughout life.
