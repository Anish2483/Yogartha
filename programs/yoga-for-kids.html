Why Yoga for Children?

Give Your Child the Gift of Inner Balance

Children are naturally joyful, open, and curious — and yoga, when taught in the right spirit, amplifies these qualities rather than suppressing them. Isha Hatha Yoga for children is not about imposing discipline or teaching complex postures — it is about helping children discover their own body, breath, and inner experience through play, movement, and wonder.

Sadhguru emphasizes that not all adult yoga practices are appropriate for children due to their still-developing skeletal and physiological systems. The Isha children’s program carefully selects and adapts practices specifically for each age group, ensuring both safety and effectiveness.

The results are profound — children who practice regularly show improvements in concentration, emotional balance, immunity, and an overall sense of joy and groundedness that supports them both in school and in life.

Benefits for Children

How Yoga Transforms Young Lives

🧠

Better Concentration & Memory

Regular practice dramatically improves a child’s ability to focus, retain information, and engage deeply with studies and creative activities.

💪

Physical Development

Supports healthy body development — flexibility, strength, posture, and coordination — laying a foundation for lifelong physical health.

🌸

Emotional Balance

Children become less reactive, more emotionally stable, and better able to manage stress, peer pressure, and challenging situations.

⚖

Stronger Immunity

Specific practices stimulate and strengthen the immune system, reducing susceptibility to common childhood illnesses.

🌿

Prevents Chronic Conditions

Early yoga practice can prevent or significantly reduce obesity, asthma, sinusitis, and other modern lifestyle-linked conditions.

⭐

Inner Joy & Self-Confidence

Children develop a natural sense of ease and confidence in their own body and mind — a gift that lasts a lifetime.

The Practices

What Children Learn at Yogartha

01
Surya Shakti (Age 8+)

18 powerful postures — an active form of sun salutation that builds strength, flexibility, and endurance in a dynamic, engaging sequence.

02
Upa Yoga (Age 7+)

Joint activation and energy practices — fun, accessible, and quickly energising. Perfect as a morning or after-school routine.

03
Yoga Namaskar (Age 7+)

A foundational yogic process that activates the lumbar spine, balances the system, and instils a sense of gratitude and reverence.

04
AUMkar Meditation (Age 7+)

A sound-based meditation using the primordial sound AUM — proven to enhance focus, memory, and mind-body coordination.

05
Nadi Shuddhi (Age 7+)

A simple alternate-nostril breathing technique that balances the energy system and brings calm, clarity, and relaxation.

06
Nada Yoga (Age 4+)

The yoga of sound — chanting, rhythm, and movement — the gentlest entry point for the youngest children into the world of yoga.
