What is the Eye Care Workshop?

Yogic Science for Healthy Eyes

The Isha Yoga Eye Care Workshop offers a comprehensive set of yogic practices specifically designed to maintain and improve the health of the eyes. In an age where most people spend the majority of their waking hours looking at screens, the eyes have become one of the most strained and neglected organ systems.

This workshop combines classical yogic eye exercises with the powerful practice of Shanmukhi Mudra — a simple but subtle practice that brightens and rejuvenates the face and eyes. Together, these practices address the eyes on both physical and energetic levels.

The practices taught in this workshop are drawn from an ancient yogic tradition of caring for the sense organs — understanding that sharp, healthy senses are not only a matter of comfort but a fundamental support for one's inner life and awareness.

Benefits

Why Join the Eye Care Workshop

Strengthens Eye Muscles

Specific yogic exercises for the eyes systematically strengthen the muscles that control eye movement and focus, improving overall visual acuity.

Reduces Eye Strain

Provides immediate and lasting relief from digital eye strain, headaches, and eye fatigue caused by prolonged screen exposure.

Brightens Eyes & Face

Through Shanmukhi Mudra and specific eye practices, the eyes and facial complexion become brighter, clearer, and more radiant.

Enhances Awareness

Healthy, well-exercised eyes support a heightened quality of awareness and perception — the foundation for all inner growth.

Slows Deterioration

Regular practice can slow the natural deterioration of eyesight associated with aging and overuse, maintaining better vision for longer.

Complements All Practices

Good visual health supports every dimension of life and yoga practice — from reading to meditation, from driving to deep inner states.
