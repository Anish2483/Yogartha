What is Surya Kriya?

A Complete Spiritual Sadhana

Surya Kriya is a potent 21-step yogic practice of immense antiquity — designed as a holistic process for health, wellbeing, and complete inner transformation. Unlike the common Sun Salutation (Surya Namaskar), Surya Kriya is an entirely different and far more comprehensive practice.

In the yogic tradition, the sun is not just the external star — it is a living presence, a source of tremendous intelligence and energy. Surya Kriya is designed to make your body and being in alignment with the solar system and harness the sun's energy within you.

Practiced regularly, Surya Kriya activates the solar plexus, builds mental focus and clarity, develops a stable emotional foundation, and prepares the system for deeper meditative states. It is a complete sadhana (spiritual practice) in itself.

The Practice

What Surya Kriya Involves

01
21 Specific Steps

A precise sequence of 21 steps involving specific body positions, breath patterns, and inner focus points.

02
Pranayama Integration

Each step incorporates specific breathing techniques (pranayama) that transform the practice into a pranic workout.

03
Bandhas (Energy Locks)

Classical bandhas are incorporated within the practice to contain and redirect the prana generated within.

04
Preparatory Asanas

Specific warm-up postures to open and prepare the body for the intensity and precision of the kriya.

05
Mantras & Chanting

Opening mantras invoke the sun's energy and create the right inner atmosphere for the practice.

06
Theory & Understanding

Reeta explains the yogic science behind each step so you practice with full understanding and not mere imitation.
