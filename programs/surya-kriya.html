What is Surya Kriya?

A Complete Spiritual Sadhana

Surya Kriya is a potent yogic practice of tremendous antiquity, designed as a holistic process for health, wellness, and complete inner wellbeing. It is much more than a physical exercise — it is a complete spiritual sadhana in itself.

Unlike Surya Namaskar, which is essentially a physical practice, Surya Kriya activates the solar plexus, balances a person's left and right energy channels (ida and pingala), and leads to a meditative state of consciousness.

Practicing Surya Kriya establishes a man in a place of health, dynamism, and clarity. Due to its intense nature, it is always taught in a proper format with appropriate preparation.

Benefits

Why Practice Surya Kriya

Physical Wellness

Helps maintain healthy weight and stimulates the physical body to reach its peak condition, building strength and vitality from within.

Relieves Stress

Generates inner fire that burns away accumulated physical and mental tension, leaving the practitioner in a state of ease and lightness.

Balances the Energy System

Balances a person's Ida and Pingala — the two primary energy channels — and leads to a heightened state of awareness and mental clarity.

Stabilizes the Spine

Brings tremendous stability, health, and suppleness to the spine. The spine is the very basis of one's physical and mental health.

Prepares for Spiritual Journey

As a complete spiritual practice in itself, Surya Kriya enhances perception and prepares the body and mind for deeper dimensions of inner exploration.

Brings Meditative Quality

Creates a meditative quality of life. Meditation is not a practice — it is a quality of being that Surya Kriya gradually cultivates.
