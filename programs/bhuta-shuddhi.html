What is Bhuta Shuddhi?

Purify the Very Elements of Your Being

"Bhuta" means the five elements — earth, water, fire, air, and space (ether). "Shuddhi" means to cleanse or purify. The human body itself is made of these five elements, and all physical and energetic imbalances arise from disturbances in these elements.

Bhuta Shuddhi is one of the most foundational and ancient of all yogic practices. Traditionally, it required intense and prolonged sadhana to access. Sadhguru has structured it in a way that makes it accessible to modern practitioners — and once learned in person, it becomes a simple 15-minute daily practice.

By systematically purifying the five elements within, this process creates the foundation for the human system to function at its peak — physically, mentally, and energetically. It is a prerequisite foundation for accessing more advanced states of energy and consciousness.

The Practice

How Bhuta Shuddhi Works

01
Earth Element (Prithvi) Cleansing

Specific processes to purify the earth element — associated with the solidity, bones, and foundational stability of the body.

02
Water Element (Jala) Cleansing

Purification of the water element governing fluids, emotions, and the fluid harmony of the body.

03
Fire Element (Agni) Cleansing

Activation and purification of the fire element — governing metabolism, transformation, and inner heat.

04
Air Element (Vayu) Cleansing

Purification of the air element governing breath, movement, circulation, and communication within the system.

05
Space Element (Akash) Cleansing

Opening and purification of the space element — the subtlest, associated with consciousness and the infinite.

06
