What is Jala Neti?

Clear Breathing, Clear Mind

Jala Neti is a traditional yogic nasal cleansing process that is part of the ancient Shatkarmas (six purification practices). It involves pouring lukewarm, saline water through one nostril and allowing it to flow out through the other using a specially designed copper Neti pot.

While simple in concept, the Isha Foundation emphasizes that this is a delicate process that must be properly imparted by a trained teacher. Improper practice or incorrect drying of the nasal passages can cause damage or reduce the benefits.

When learned correctly, Jala Neti is highly effective in clearing accumulated mucus and pollutants, providing immense relief for asthma, allergies, and sinusitis, while simultaneously calming the nervous system.
