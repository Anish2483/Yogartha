What is Angamardhana?

Full Mastery Over the Body

Angamardhana, meaning “gaining complete mastery over the limbs, organs, and other parts of the body,” is a fitness system rooted in yoga that revitalizes the body on every level — muscles, circulatory system, skeletal structure, nervous system, and the basic energy system.

This powerful system uses no equipment at all — the only thing needed is a 6-by-6-foot space. Yet the range of processes it contains has the potential to put you in a state of physical fitness and energy that most modern exercise systems cannot match.

Angamardhana works on the body in a fundamentally different way from modern fitness — it activates the entire human system, not just the muscles, bringing about a state of physical health, vitality, and inner aliveness that is remarkable.

The Practice

What Angamardhana Involves

01
Dynamic Processes

A set of vigorous, dynamic processes that use only body weight, working every part of the body systematically and powerfully.

02
No Equipment

All you need is a 6×6 foot space. This makes Angamardhana supremely accessible and practical for any lifestyle.

03
Progressive Intensity

Beginners start with foundational processes and progressively advance, allowing any fitness level to benefit safely and fully.

04
Breath Coordination

Each process is performed with specific breath coordination, amplifying physical work into a full pranic workout for the energy body.

05
Classical Transmission

Taught exactly as transmitted through the Isha tradition — with the understanding of how each process impacts the human system.
