What is Isha Hatha Yoga?

A Technology for Inner Wellbeing

Isha Hatha Yoga programs are offered as a systematic practice, providing a holistic foundation in Hatha Yoga under the guidance of Sadhguru. These classical practices are powerful systems for inner growth — they support one's spiritual journey and can even become a complete spiritual path in themselves.

The Hatha Yoga system transmitted by Isha Foundation is rooted in a profound understanding of the human system — physical, mental, energetic — and is designed to bring each dimension to its peak. Practiced with integrity and regularity, these processes bring the body and mind to a state of extraordinary possibility.

At Yogartha, Reeta teaches these systems with the same authenticity and depth she received during her training at the Sadhguru Gurukulam — with absolute faithfulness to how Sadhguru intended them to be transmitted.

The Six Core Systems

Classical Hatha Yoga Practices

Upa Yoga

A simple yet effective system that activates the joints, muscles, and energy system. An ideal introduction to the yogic process.

Surya Kriya

A potent yogic practice of tremendous antiquity, designed as a holistic process for health, wellness, and complete inner wellbeing.

Yogasanas

A set of 36 powerful postures to enable the system to sustain higher dimensions of energy, transforming body, mind, and the very way you experience life.

Angamardhana

A fitness system that brings full vigor to the body — using no equipment, just the human body alone, to build strength, agility, and endurance.

Bhuta Shuddhi

A process of cleansing the five elements — earth, water, fire, air, and space — that make up the body, for fundamental wellbeing and inner harmony.

Surya Shakti

A dynamic form of sun salutation, a powerful physical conditioning practice that builds the physical body and creates a basis of energy and stability.
