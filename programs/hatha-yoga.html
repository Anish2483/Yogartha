The Full Program

Yoga in Its Full Depth & Vibrancy

Isha Hatha Yoga is not a modified or fitness-oriented adaptation of yoga — it is classical yoga preserved exactly as it has been transmitted for thousands of years. Not just as exercise, not just as stress relief, but as a complete science for mastering the human body and mind.

The body is the most sophisticated instrument available to human beings. When properly prepared through classical Hatha Yoga, it becomes a perfect vehicle for inner exploration and outer excellence. Each of the six core practices within Isha's Hatha Yoga tradition targets a different dimension of the human system.

At Yogartha, Reeta teaches these six practices with the same authenticity, precision, and depth she received during her Sadhguru Gurukulam training. Daily batches are available from 6 AM, with evening sessions also available.

The Six Core Systems

What's Included in the Program

🧘

Upa Yoga

10 simple practices for joint activation and energy flow. The ideal starting point — suitable for absolute beginners from day one.

☀️

Surya Kriya

A potent 21-step yogic sun process that activates the solar plexus and builds remarkable mental and physical stability.

🌿

Yogasanas

36 classical postures practiced with meditative awareness — a complete technology for body-energy-mind alignment.

⚡

Angamardana

31 powerful dynamic processes using only body weight — builds peak physical fitness, agility, and strength without equipment.

🌍

Bhuta Shuddhi

Purification of the five elements within — creates fundamental harmony and mastery at the deepest level of the human system.

🔆

Surya Shakti

An 18-step dynamic sun process that builds the physical body's strength, flexibility, and musculoskeletal endurance.

Program Structure

How the Program Works at Yogartha

01
Daily Morning Batches (6 AM)

Six-days-a-week morning batches covering the complete Hatha Yoga curriculum progressively. Ideal for committed practitioners.

02
Evening Group Sessions (6 PM)

Accessible evening sessions for working professionals and those unable to attend mornings. All levels welcome.

Private & Personalised Sessions

One-on-one sessions with Reeta for tailored guidance, specific health goals, or faster progression through the curriculum.

Theory & Understanding

Regular sessions explaining the yogic science behind each practice — you practice with understanding, not just imitation.

05
Mantra, Ritual & Sacred Atmosphere

Every class begins with classical chanting and a lamp-lighting ritual, creating the right inner space for transformation.

06
Ongoing Support

Access to Reeta for questions, corrections, and guidance beyond class hours. A supportive community of fellow practitioners.
