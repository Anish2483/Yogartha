Yoga Beyond the Studio

Bringing Classical Yoga to Where You Are

Yogartha brings the authentic science of Classical Hatha Yoga directly to corporates, schools, hospitals, government institutions, residential societies, and community organizations — across Dehradun and beyond into different cities.

These are not generic ‘corporate wellness’ sessions. Every program delivered by Yogartha is rooted in the classical Isha tradition, led by Reeta — a certified teacher trained under the prestigious Sadhguru Gurukulam. Sessions are structured to be immediately impactful while remaining true to the depth of the original practice.

Whether it’s a single wellness day, a recurring weekly program, or a city-wide initiative — Yogartha can design a program that fits your organization’s needs, schedule, and objectives.

Benefits

What Yoga Does for Organizations

Reduced Stress & Burnout

Classical Hatha Yoga practices reduce cortisol, lower blood pressure, and significantly reduce burnout in high-pressure work environments.

Enhanced Focus & Productivity

Employees who practice yoga report sharper focus, better decision-making, and significantly higher output — measurable ROI for organizations.

Team Cohesion

Shared practice creates a rare sense of connection and trust among team members — improving collaboration, communication, and morale.

Physical Wellbeing

Reduces musculoskeletal issues from desk work, improves posture, energy levels, and reduces sick leave — directly impacting organizational health costs.

Mental Health Support

Provides employees with powerful, self-managed tools for managing anxiety, sleep, and emotional regulation — without dependency on external interventions.

Customized Delivery

Programs are tailored to your organization’s timing, space, and objectives — from early morning sessions to full wellness days across cities.
