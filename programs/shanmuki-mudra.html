What is Shanmukhi Mudra?

A Gateway to Inner Stillness

Shanmukhi Mudra is a simple but subtle practice that brightens and rejuvenates the face and eyes. The word “Shanmukhi” refers to the six gateways of perception — the two eyes, two ears, nose, and mouth — and this mudra involves a specific arrangement of the fingers to seal these senses, withdrawing awareness from the external world.

In the yogic tradition, the senses are described as pointing outward by nature — they are designed to encounter the external world. Shanmukhi Mudra turns this orientation inward, creating a profound stillness and inner spaciousness that is difficult to access through other means.

This simple yet powerful practice is an integral part of the Isha Yoga Eye Care Workshop and can be practiced independently as a rejuvenating process for the sense organs, particularly the eyes and face.

The Practice

What Shanmukhi Mudra Involves

01
Specific Finger Arrangement

A particular placement of the fingers that seals the six gateways of perception, turning attention inward with precision and care.

02
Inner Sound Awareness

With the ears sealed, awareness is directed to inner sound — a gateway to deeper dimensions of stillness and inner experience.

03
Breath Awareness

Practiced with specific breath awareness, the mudra becomes a complete inner practice — body, breath, and attention aligned inward.
