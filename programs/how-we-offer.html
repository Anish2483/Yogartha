Beyond Regular Classes

Deep Immersion Into the Yogic Way

Yogartha offers practices through diverse formats that go beyond the regular class format. Spanning from a single focused day to multi-day residential camps, these sessions create a dedicated container for participants to dive deep into Classical Hatha Yoga practices.

Each offering is structured around a specific practice or theme — from a foundational Hatha Yoga camp for beginners to advanced Surya Kriya intensives, seasonal cleansing camps, or themed workshops on pranayama, meditation, and yogic philosophy. Every session is led by Reeta, trained under the Sadhguru Gurukulam.

Whether it’s a weekend retreat in the Himalayas, a local day workshop at the Yogartha shala, or a special program for a festival or occasion — each offering is an opportunity for a genuine, lasting shift in your practice and your life.

Benefits

Why an Intensive Offering?

Deep Immersion

Extended sessions allow practices to settle into the body and mind at a depth that regular weekly classes simply cannot achieve.

Community & Connection

Shared intensity creates powerful bonds — participants often describe camps as transformative not just personally, but communally.

Accelerated Progress

A focused, uninterrupted block of practice accelerates progress in ways that months of regular classes cannot match.

Seasonal Alignment

Camps are often timed around seasonal transitions — the ideal time for deep cleansing and renewal of the human system.

Himalayan Setting

Select offerings are conducted in the natural environment of the Himalayas, amplifying the quality and depth of every practice.

All Levels Welcome

From complete beginners to experienced practitioners — these formats are structured so everyone can participate and grow meaningfully.
