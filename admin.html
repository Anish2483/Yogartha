ImgBB — Free Image Hosting
What is this?
ImgBB is a free image hosting service. When you upload a photo in the Gallery or Testimonials section, this app sends it to ImgBB and stores the link in your database. It is completely free — no credit card needed.

How to get your free API key:
1. Go to imgbb.com → Sign Up (free)
2. After logging in, go to imgbb.com/api
3. Click "Get API Key"
4. Copy the key and paste it below
