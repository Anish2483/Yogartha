Our Sacred Space

Bringing Isha's Classical
Hatha Yoga to Dehradun

Yogartha is a Classical Hatha Yoga studio rooted in the heart of Dehradun — a space where the ancient science of yoga is not diluted, not decorative, but delivered in its purest form. Led by a certified teacher trained under the prestigious Sadhguru Gurukulam, every session here is a faithful transmission of a tradition that has endured for thousands of years.

Our Mission — To nurture holistic well-being by offering Classical Hatha Yoga exactly as Sadhguru has structured it. We walk with each student on a deeply personal journey toward physical vitality, mental clarity, and spiritual balance — empowering them to discover the transformative power that lives within their own body and breath.

Founder & Your Guide

Reeta

Isha Hatha Yoga Teacher · Founder, Yogartha · Dehradun, Uttarakhand

After dedicating over 13 years to the financial sector, I began to notice how many people around me were struggling with stress, anxiety, and a sense of unrest. Amidst the fast-paced, success-driven lifestyle, I felt something essential was missing — a sense of peace, vitality, and genuine joy in daily life. This realization inspired me to seek a path that could bring balance and fulfillment, not only to myself but also to others.

This question led me to explore yoga more deeply, and I eventually discovered Hatha Yoga — an ancient practice focused on cultivating balance, clarity, and vitality. Inspired by Sadhguru's vision of making Hatha Yoga accessible to everyone in its original purity and teaching it as a transformative process, I decided to undergo Isha Hatha Yoga Teacher Training. My goal is not only to transform my own life but also to help others find a path to peace and well-being. Teaching this pure, classical form of yoga has given me a profound sense of purpose, and I am passionate about sharing its transformative benefits.

🎵

Outside of yoga, I love exploring new things — creative pursuits, outdoor adventures, and wholehearted involvement in whatever I do. From a young age, I enjoyed singing, dancing, and playing sports. I am a classical singer and dancer by passion, finding beauty in the involvement, discipline, and rhythm of these art forms. Life, to me, is about embracing every moment with enthusiasm.
