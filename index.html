Our Sacred Space

Bringing Isha's Classical
Yoga to Dehradun

Yogartha is a classical Hatha Yoga studio nestled in the foothills of Dehradun, inspired by the profound teachings of the Isha Foundation and Sadhguru Jaggi Vasudev. We offer the ancient science of Hatha Yoga exactly as it was transmitted — with precision, reverence, and depth.

Here, yoga is not fitness. It is a systematic technology to bring the body and mind to their peak possibility — and to go beyond.

Your Guide

Reeta

Isha Hatha Yoga Teacher · Dehradun, Uttarakhand

Reeta has been walking the path of yoga since her early teens. Drawn to the depth and precision of the Isha tradition, she completed her Hatha Yoga Teacher Training at the Isha Institute of Inner Sciences and returned home to Dehradun to share this sacred science.

She brings to every class a rare combination of warmth and discipline. Her students describe her sessions as transformative — not just for the body, but for their entire way of being. Reeta teaches with patience, heart, and unwavering dedication to the classical form.
