Who We Are

YOGARTHA

YOGARTHA is a Classical Hatha Yoga studio located in Dehradun, led by a certified Hatha Yoga teacher trained under the prestigious Sadhguru Gurukulam. The Science

What is Classical
Hatha Yoga?

Hatha Yoga is one of the most powerful and complete systems for human wellbeing ever devised. Unlike modern fitness-oriented yoga, Classical Hatha Yoga is a precise, time-tested science — structured by Sadhguru — that works on the physical body as a doorway to inner dimensions.

Through specific postures, breathing techniques, and energetic practices, it purifies the system, builds vitality, and brings the body and mind into a state of perfect receptivity — making it a complete spiritual path, not merely an exercise.

“Hatha Yoga is not about being flexible or fit — it is about making yourself into a perfect vessel to receive and reflect the Divine.” — Sadhguru

Surya Kriya

Surya Kriya is a potent yogic practice. It is of tremendous antiquity, designed as a holistic process for health, wellness, and complete inner wellbeing. “Surya” means “sun,” and “kriya” means “inner energy process.”

Yogasanas

A Technology for Inner Transformation
The word asana literally means a posture. Out of the innumerable asanas a body can assume, 84 have been identified as Yogasanas, through which one can transform the body and mind into a possibility for ultimate wellbeing.

Angamardhana

Full Mastery Over the Body
Angamardana, a fitness system rooted in yoga, offers everyone the opportunity to invigorate the body and reach peak physical and mental health. “Angamardana” means gaining complete mastery over the limbs, organs, and other parts of the body.

Bhuta Shuddhi

Purification of the Five Elements
The basis for all creation, including the physical body, is the group of five elements: earth, water, wind, fire, and space. The wellbeing of the body and mind can be established by purifying these five elements within the human system.

Upa Yoga

Upa Yoga is a simple yet powerful set of 10 practices that activate the joints, muscles and energy system, bringing ease to the whole system. Based on a sophisticated understanding of the body’s mechanics, Upa Yoga dispels inertia in the body’s energy and brings ease to the whole system.

Shanmuki Mudra

Shanmukhi Mudra is a simple but subtle practice that brightens and rejuvenates the face and eyes and brings about a state of balance leading toward increased awareness and meditativeness.

Jala Neti

Cleansing the Nasal Passage
Jala Neti is one of the six classical yogic shatkarmas — cleansing processes — that form an important foundation of the Hatha Yoga tradition.

Founder & Your Guide

Reeta

Classical Hatha Yoga Teacher · Founder, Yogartha ·, Uttarakhand

After dedicating over 13 years to the financial sector, I began to notice how many people around me were struggling with stress, anxiety, and a sense of unrest. Amidst the fast-paced, success-driven lifestyle, I felt something essential was missing — a sense of peace, vitality, and genuine joy in daily life. This realization inspired me to seek a path that could bring balance and fulfillment, not only to myself but also to others.

This question led me to explore yoga more deeply, and I eventually discovered Hatha Yoga — an ancient practice focused on cultivating balance, clarity, and vitality. Inspired by Sadhguru's vision of making Hatha Yoga accessible to everyone in its original purity and teaching it as a transformative process, I decided to undergo Classical Hatha Yoga Teacher Training. My goal is not only to transform my own life but also to help others find a path to peace and well-being. Teaching this pure, classical form of yoga has given me a profound sense of purpose, and I am passionate about sharing its transformative benefits.

Outside of yoga, I love exploring new things — creative pursuits, outdoor adventures, and wholehearted involvement in whatever I do. From a young age, I enjoyed singing, dancing, and playing sports. I am a classical singer and dancer by passion, finding beauty in the involvement, discipline, and rhythm of these art forms. Life, to me, is about embracing every moment with enthusiasm.
